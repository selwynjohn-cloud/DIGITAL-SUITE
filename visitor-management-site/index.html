Visitor Desk

Know who is at the gate — without the chaos

A calm visitor management system for reception desks, societies, and campuses. Register visitors, notify hosts, and print or share a pass in seconds.

Request early access See how it works

How it works

  1. Visitor arrives Reception enters name, purpose, and host.
  2. Host is notified WhatsApp or SMS alert with approve / hold options.
  3. Pass is issued Time-stamped gate pass — on screen, print, or phone.
  4. Exit is logged Clear in/out record for the day.

What you get

  • Fast check-in for walk-ins and expected guests
  • Host approval before entry
  • Photo & ID capture (optional)
  • Daily visitor log and search
  • Multi-gate / multi-desk ready
  • Works on phone and desktop
