## 1. Major Bank Heist & Financial Crime

**₹4-Crore Gold & ₹10 Lakh Cash Bank Heist in Giridih — DVR Stolen (Jharkhand):** Five armed robbers—one wearing a helmet and four in masks—stormed a bank branch less than 200 metres from the Dhanwar police station in Giridih around 1:40 PM on Thursday. Holding branch manager Rahul Kumar Gaur, three staffers, a contractual employee, and two customers at gunpoint for 20 minutes, the gang bound their hands and mouths with plastic tape, assaulted a customer who resisted, looted gold ornaments worth ₹3–4 crore and ₹10 lakh in cash from the strongroom locker, and disconnected and carried away the CCTV DVR boxes before locking the main entrance from outside and fleeing. Giridih SP Bimal Kumar confirmed special police teams are conducting raids.

**Jamtara Cyber Fraud Crackdown (Jharkhand):** Jamtara Police arrested three cyber criminals and seized ₹8.13 lakh in cash during an anti-cyber-fraud raid.
