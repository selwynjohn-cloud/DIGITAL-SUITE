eGate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 · egate.co.in

eGate is a personal visitor and gate management app by John Selwyn. This policy explains what the app handles.

Data we collect

When you check in a visitor, you may enter name, host, purpose, and optional phone number. In the current app version, this information is stored only on your device in memory and is cleared when the app is closed or restarted. It is not uploaded to our servers.

How we use data

Visitor details are used only to show a gate pass and today’s list inside the app on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. If you use the Share action, the content you choose to share leaves the device through your phone’s share sheet (for example WhatsApp or Messages) under that service’s terms.

Third parties

App distribution uses Apple App Store and Google Play. Their privacy policies apply to account and device data they collect.

Children

eGate is intended for workplace and residential gate use by adults. It is not directed at children under 13.
